Built for Diverse Minds

Key Features Designed for Success

Every tool is crafted to remove barriers to reading, understanding, and independent learning.

🔊

Accurate Phonics Models

Teaches letter sounds vs letter names clearly. Learn /m/ as in moon, /ʃ/ as in ship, and blend sounds with smooth visual audio highlights.

🪟

Reading Ruler & Focus Mode

An interactive floating reading line guides the reader's eye, preventing visual crowding and line skipping.

🎨

Calm & High-Contrast Themes

Switch seamlessly between cheerful light pastel, eye-friendly soft cream (sepia), and high-contrast dark mode.

🔤

Dyslexia-Friendly Typography

Adjust font size, line spacing, letter spacing, and toggle between friendly Nunito and specialized weighted font styling.

🎧

Interactive Audio Player

Read-aloud with speed controls (0.75x to 1.5x), word-by-word karaoke highlighting, and click-to-pronounce on any unfamiliar word.

📊

Encouraging Analytics

Progress metrics celebrate effort, streaks, and practice time without punitive grading or stressful timers.

Inclusive Design

Accessibility & Display Controls

Personalize your reading experience. All settings save automatically in your browser.

🎨 Color Theme

Select a palette that is gentle on your eyes and offers comfortable contrast.

🔠 Text Size

Adjust reading text size across all lessons, stories, and flashcards.

📏 Line Spacing

More space between lines prevents visual crowding and accidental line skipping.

↔️ Letter Spacing

Widening letter spacing can significantly improve readability for many learners.

🔤 Font Family

Choose between clean modern, friendly rounded, or weighted dyslexia-friendly fonts.

👁️ Reading Focus Ruler

A translucent guide line follows your cursor or finger to keep your place.

🛑 Reduce Animations

Disables floating animations and bouncy transitions for a calmer experience.

🔊 Sound Effects & Audio Chimes

Play cheerful chimes when answering correctly or interacting with cards.
